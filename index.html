1. How to Make a Fresher CV

Include your full name, professional contact details, career objective, education, skills, projects, internship experience and relevant achievements. Use clear headings and simple formatting. Never include false qualifications or experience.

2. Interview Preparation for Freshers

Research the employer and role. Practise a 30–60 second self-introduction covering your education, relevant skills and career goal. Prepare examples from college projects or training, and carry the documents requested by the employer.

3. How to Check a Job Notification

Read the notification on the employer's official website. Verify the post name, eligibility, age limit, salary details, application fee, deadline and selection process. Avoid relying only on forwarded messages or unofficial posts.

4. Job Application Checklist

Before submitting, check your name, date of birth, education details, phone number and email. Prepare the required photo, signature, certificates and payment receipt if applicable. Save the final application and acknowledgement for your records.

5. Free Digital Skills to Learn

Start with email writing, document formatting, spreadsheets, online research, video meetings and professional communication. Practise with small projects. Use official learning resources and never pay an unknown person who promises guaranteed employment.

6. How to Identify Job Scams

Be cautious if a recruiter demands money to guarantee a job, uses an unofficial email address, avoids interviews or pressures you to act immediately. Verify vacancies through the employer's official website and never share OTPs, passwords or banking PINs.
