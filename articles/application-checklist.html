Job Application Checklist

Use this checklist before submitting a job application. Requirements vary by employer, so always follow the official notification.

1. Personal Information

  • ☐ Full name matches your documents
  • ☐ Date of birth is correct
  • ☐ Phone number and email are active
  • ☐ Address and other required details are accurate

2. Education and Experience

  • ☐ Required qualification completed or expected as permitted
  • ☐ Marksheets and certificates are available
  • ☐ Experience or internship details are accurate
  • ☐ Professional registration is available if required

3. Upload Documents

  • ☐ Recent photograph meets the specified size
  • ☐ Signature meets the required format
  • ☐ Certificates are clear and readable
  • ☐ Category or domicile certificates are included if required

4. Review the Application

  • ☐ Check eligibility and age limits
  • ☐ Review every field before submitting
  • ☐ Confirm the deadline and application fee
  • ☐ Pay only through the official application portal

5. After Submission

  • ☐ Download or print the submitted application
  • ☐ Save the acknowledgement or registration number
  • ☐ Keep the payment receipt if applicable
  • ☐ Check the official website for further updates

Important Safety Tip

Never share your password, OTP or banking PIN with a recruiter. Do not pay anyone who promises guaranteed selection.
