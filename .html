How to Make a Fresher CV

A CV introduces your education, skills and experience to an employer. A fresher can create a professional CV even without full-time work experience.

1. Personal Details

  • Full name
  • Professional phone number and email address
  • City and state
  • LinkedIn profile, if available

2. Career Objective

Example: “Motivated graduate seeking an entry-level opportunity to apply my knowledge, develop professional skills and contribute to the organisation.”

3. Education

List your qualification, college or university, passing year and marks or CGPA accurately.

4. Skills

Include relevant skills such as MS Word, Excel, communication, documentation, teamwork or technical knowledge related to the job.

5. Projects and Training

Mention college projects, internships, industrial training, certifications and practical work that relate to the position.

6. CV Checklist

  • Check spelling and contact details.
  • Use clear headings and consistent formatting.
  • Keep information truthful and relevant.
  • Save the final CV as a PDF unless another format is requested.
  • Use a suitable file name, such as YourName_CV.pdf.

Final Tip

Adjust your CV to the job description. Highlight the skills and qualifications that match the position.
